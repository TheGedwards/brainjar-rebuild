# Blog Content Plan — Topic Clusters & Internal Linking

_Content-velocity strategy for brainjarmedia.com. Started 2026-07-27._

## The model: pillars + clusters

Search engines reward topical authority. Instead of scattered one-off posts, we
group content into **clusters** that each support a **pillar page**:

- **Pillars = the four service pages** (`/services/seo`, `/web-development`,
  `/content-marketing`, `/paid-advertising`). These are the pages we actually
  want to rank and convert on.
- **Clusters = blog posts** that answer specific questions within that pillar's
  topic. Each post links **up** to its pillar; each pillar links **down** to its
  posts. That link structure is what tells Google the pillar is authoritative.

## How it's wired (already built)

Every post carries a **Service pillar** (the `category` field in the blog
editor — set it to SEO / Web Development / Content Marketing / Paid Advertising).
That single field drives:

- **Post → pillar:** a "Filed under [Service]" link at the end of each post.
- **Pillar → posts:** a "More on [Service]" section on each service page listing
  its cluster posts.
- **Post → post:** a "Keep Reading" block showing related posts (same pillar
  first, then most recent).

**Rule of thumb when writing:** always set the pillar, and work in 1–2 contextual
links inside the body — to the relevant service or sub-service page, a location
page, or a case study. Aim for a link every ~300 words. Never leave a post an
orphan.

## Cadence

Consistency beats bursts. Target **2–4 posts per month**, rotating across
pillars so each cluster grows. A steady drumbeat of useful, well-linked posts is
the single biggest remaining SEO lever now that the technical foundation is done.

## Topic backlog

Concrete post ideas, each tied to a real search intent. Titles are drafts — keep
them ≤60 characters and answer the searcher's question. Local angles (Gresham,
Portland, East County, Troutdale, Sandy…) also feed the location pages.

### Pillar: SEO (`seo`)
- "How Long Does SEO Take to Work?" — sets expectations; high-intent question.
- "Local SEO Checklist for Gresham Businesses" — local + actionable.
- "Google Business Profile: The Free Local SEO Win" — plays to your 69 reviews.
- "Why 'Near Me' Searches Matter for East County Shops" — local intent.
- "What Actually Moves Rankings in [year]" — evergreen, refresh annually.
- "SEO vs. Paid Ads: Which Should You Start With?" — cross-links Paid pillar.

### Pillar: Web Development (`web`)
- "Signs Your Website Is Costing You Customers" — pain-aware, converts.
- "How Fast Should Your Website Be? (Core Web Vitals)" — technical, credible.
- "Do You Own Your Website? Why It Matters" — echoes a service FAQ.
- "WordPress vs. a Custom Build: An Honest Comparison" — decision-stage.
- "What a Small-Business Website Should Cost in Oregon" — pricing intent + local.
- "Redesign or Rebuild? How to Tell" — links to case studies.

### Pillar: Content Marketing (`content`)
- "How Often Should a Small Business Blog?" — meta, sets cadence expectations.
- "Content That Ranks AND Converts: The Difference" — core value prop.
- "Turning One Case Study into a Month of Content" — repurposing.
- "Email Isn't Dead: Making Your List Pay Off" — links Email sub-service.
- "What to Write When You 'Have Nothing to Write About'" — unblocks prospects.

### Pillar: Paid Advertising (`paid`)
- "How to Hire Someone for Google Ads" — ✅ already published.
- "What Budget Do You Need for Google Ads?" — echoes a service FAQ.
- "Google Ads vs. Facebook Ads: Where to Spend First" — decision-stage.
- "Why Your Ads Get Clicks but No Customers" — landing-page tie-in (Web pillar).
- "Retargeting 101 for Local Businesses" — approachable, local.

## Measuring it
Watch **Admin → Analytics** (Search Console) for impressions/clicks on the
pillar pages and new posts, and **Admin → SEO Health** to keep every new post
clean (title ≤60, description 70–160, one H1, internal links present).
